## Our recommendation in one line

Bellwood isn't short of patients. It overpays for temporary staff and loses patients who have already been referred to it. Our plan is to fix that first, using the $35M budget, and it reaches the board's **7% operating margin within 18 months**.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Operating margin | 4.0% today → **7.0% by month 18** |
| Operating income | **+$71M a year** (base case) |
| Payback on the $35M | **About 6 months** |
| More patients seen a year | **About 10,600** |

## The solution: the AI front door

- **Books every referral** into the earliest suitable slot at any Bellwood clinic, instead of only the clinic it was sent to.
- **Follows up** with the patient so fewer drop out, and **reports back** to the family doctor.
- **Forecasts demand** a few weeks ahead from past booking data, so busy periods are covered by Bellwood's own float-pool staff instead of expensive agency staff.
- **Plans beds as one system** across the 3 hospitals.
- **A manager approves** every staffing and capacity change. The AI recommends, and people decide.
